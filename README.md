# Consecutive-equal-sum-partitions
We studied the least length of a finite indexed family of positive real numbers that admits equal sum-partitions into a fixed number of consecutive group counts.

To be specific, fix an integer $r\ge 3$. For a positive integer $m$, let $f_r(m)$ be the least length of a finite indexed family of positive real numbers of total sum $1$ that admits equal-sum partitions into $m,m+1,\ldots,m+r-1$ nonempty groups. Writing $d=r-1$, we prove that

$$f_r(m)=2m+\Theta_r\\left(m^{\frac{r-2}{r-1}}\right)=2m+\Theta_d\\left(m^{\frac{d-1}{d}}\right)\qquad (m\to\infty).$$
